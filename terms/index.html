A GOOD DOG’S GUIDE TO THE INTERNET

Dog House rules.

Be kind. Stay curious. Leave a nice pawprint.

A public place for small conversations

The guestbook is for ideas, feedback, friendly hellos and the occasional excellent dog fact. You need to sign in with X to post. Your note and public profile details appear immediately.

Keep the welcome mat welcoming

  • Respect other people. Harassment, threats and hate have no place here.
  • Share your own thoughts, not spam or repeated promotions.
  • Keep private information private, including other people’s personal details.
  • Only share content you have the right to publish.

Posting and removing notes

Notes can be up to 1,000 characters. A short pause between posts helps keep spam down. You can delete your own notes while signed in. The site operator can remove notes that break these rules or disrupt the guestbook.

A note about Byte

Byte is a fictional dog and WoofLab’s mascot. His origin story and workshop pictures are creative fiction. The small tools in the lab are real browser experiments for you to try.
