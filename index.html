02 / BEHIND THE PAWS

Meet Byte.
The story is fictional.
The curiosity isn't.

Meet Byte, WoofLab’s fictional founder and self-appointed Chief Treat Officer.

Byte’s first invention was an automatic treat dispenser. It worked perfectly until he discovered how to hold the button down. After eating the entire research budget, he decided to try software.

He founded WoofLab under a desk, between a forgotten charging cable and his favourite tennis ball. It’s his workshop for small tools and curious experiments, supervised with a tilted head and occasional keyboard interference.

His working philosophy? Follow your curiosity. Make something useful. Take a walk when you get stuck.

Small paws. Big ideas.
Absolutely no control around snacks.
