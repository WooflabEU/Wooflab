Privacy.

The guestbook is public. Here’s what that means for your data.

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Reading the site

You can browse the site and read guestbook notes without signing in. WoofLab does not add advertising trackers or analytics scripts. Cloudflare hosts the site and may process technical request information to deliver and protect it.

Signing in with X

Supabase Auth handles sign-in through X. It stores the information needed to identify your account and maintain your session. This includes your account identifier, display name, X handle and profile picture URL. X may also provide your email address to the authentication service; it is not published in the guestbook.

Your browser stores the login session locally so you can remain signed in. Signing out removes the local session. Profile pictures are loaded from X’s image service, which receives the image request.

Your public comments

Your comment, display name, X handle, profile picture and posting time are visible to everyone. Comments may be indexed or copied by third parties. Please only post information you want to make public.

Comments are stored in the site’s Supabase database. They stay public after you sign out. While signed in, you can delete your own comments. The site operator can remove comments to maintain the guestbook.

Account removal and questions

You can remove individual notes directly in the guestbook. The site operator can also remove your authentication account; this deletes its associated profile and comments from the application database.

For privacy questions or account removal, contact the site operator.
